One new website, two ways to run it

Both demos show the same new design and the same projects. The only difference is the editor your team would use. Try the three tasks below in each, then send us your scores.

WordPress

Edit the page itself and click Save. Familiar, and widely known by freelancers.

The three tasks

1. Change the featured project on the homepage

WordPress

  1. The demo opens in the Site Editor. Close the welcome guide.
  2. Click the large featured project at the top of the page.
  3. In the right-hand panel, open "Featured project" and choose another project.
  4. Click Save, then Save again to confirm. Open the site to see it.

Decap

  1. Click "Login" (no password in the demo).
  2. Open Homepage, then Homepage settings.
  3. Choose another project under "Featured project". The preview on the right updates.
  4. Click Publish.
2. Update Himalayan Kids: status, text and photo

WordPress

  1. In the dashboard, open Projects, then Himalayan Kids.
  2. Edit the text directly on the page.
  3. Change Status in the right-hand panel, and set a photo under "Featured image".
  4. Click Save.

Decap

  1. Open Projects, then Himalayan Kids.
  2. Change Status, the page text and the Image field. Watch the preview.
  3. Click Publish.
3. Add a new project

WordPress

  1. Open Projects, then "Add new project".
  2. Write a title and some text.
  3. Tick a Strand, Kind and Status, and fill in "Project details".
  4. Publish. The project appears in the season list on the homepage.

Decap

  1. Open Projects, then "New Project".
  2. Fill in the fields. The preview shows the new program page.
  3. Click Publish.

Good to know

  • Both demos are private to your browser. Nothing you change is public or reaches PFC’s real website, so experiment freely. The Decap demo resets when you reload; the WordPress demo may remember your changes in this browser.
  • In the Decap demo, your changes show in the preview beside the form. On the real site, Publish updates the live page about a minute later.
  • Boxes that say "from PFC needed" are waiting for photos from your team.
  • Every status says "to confirm" except Himalayan Kids, until your team confirms which work is active.
